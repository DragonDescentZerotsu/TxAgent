# DILI retrieval review v4: repair first, then targeted replay

This release follows up the user-selected EMO/emodin, 4-HPPP and 3TC/fluorinated-analog defects, including matching records in their three papers. The first staged index exposed 40 replacement cards; their review found additional incorrect identities and placements, so the same-paper/identity siblings were also checked. Decisions use source records and original papers, without benchmark labels or old predictions. The inventory contains 713 source rows, not the entire library or the entire ~20k retrieved surface pool.

## Reviewed changes

`change_summary.json` is authoritative: 496 modified records; 383 remain retrieval-eligible, including 9 restored after identity repair. 85 records move between retained levels. 112 previously eligible associations are withdrawn; one already excluded material record is repaired and remains excluded. Identity/text/placement counts overlap. All 1,645,109 original rows remain; no raw record is deleted.

- EMO is corrected to emodin, and the separate RHE result to rhein. The original abstract and supplied extraction support the identity and endpoint corrections. PMID 27032576 full text was inaccessible; its numerical extraction is not claimed independently full-text verified.
- 4-HPPP is the phenoxyphenol derivative, not pyrogallol. The full PMC6154338 text distinguishes tumor efficacy from the Clone 9 comparator. Normal-cell comparisons and bounded cell-death/autophagy mechanisms are retained; pure HCC growth/nuclear-enlargement/DNA-damage efficacy endpoints are outside DILI retrieval.
- The full original JCI117698 PDF separates lamivudine/3TC from the fluorinated analog discussed through reference 39. The latter is retained as bounded secondary negative mitochondrial evidence under the corrected emtricitabine stereochemistry; it is not treated as original Table II morphology data. D-FMAU is distinguished from L-FMAU/clevudine. Mitochondrial morphology, lactate, mtDNA duration and HPLC figure attribution are corrected. The printed ATP absolute unit remains explicitly unresolved; no guessed conversion.
- Replacement/sibling repairs distinguish ANIT from an unrelated acid, geniposidic acid from GMP, clofibrate from an unrelated nitrile, AAF from a fluorouracil derivative, BP-3 from butylparaben, and glucuronide from glucoside. The AAF publisher's Chemicals section identifies its BP comparator as benzo(a)pyrene, not bisphenol A.
- The original HNG paper reports a defined peptide sequence. Its incorrect nucleoside association is repaired and its negative hepatic functional findings retained. AICAR remains the riboside used by the papers; an ambiguous PubChem abbreviation lookup returning the phosphorylated metabolite is not adopted.
- HCS16598496.pdf separately reports an initial no-effect vidarabine screen and subsequent Table 2 positive readouts. Both observations are retained with their experiment scope; the initial aggregate result is not a universal mitochondrial negative. The table image was inspected. Generic plasma-membrane blebbing, nuclear area and spheroid morphology leave L4; actual canalicular and bile-transport results remain.
- The CT paper describes a group preparation whose attached single-congener structure is unsupported. Four multi-quinone comparisons were wrongly attached to lawsone; their unique focal compound cannot be recovered without inventing assignments. Corrected material/multi-subject records remain stored while these false single-molecule associations are quarantined. A separate explicitly menadione-focused record is repaired and retained.
- Chang Liver and SMMC-7721 are documented HeLa derivatives (Cellosaurus CVCL_0238/CVCL_0534). Generic viability/morphology is not retained as hepatic function. The bounded BP-3 redox mechanism retains the corrected model limitation. Nonhepatic negative, protective and indirect mechanisms are not automatically excluded.

## Validation and inference gates

`content_repairs.json` binds each decision to source UID, raw payload hash and previous level; `individual_review.jsonl` records all inventory dispositions. `source_validation.json` checks all raw rows, every unmodified row and 27 frozen gold/split/vote files. `staged_validation.json` records the current scaffold index identity/heldout validation. `replay_after_scaffold_checks.py` waits for the build to finish and writes `scaffold_replay_release.json` to authorize isolated scaffold preparation/replay after these gates; it does not publish the source globally. The shared L1-only heldout prefilter, L2 retention and query-time disjoint remain unchanged; no historical voter was modified.

`selected_surface_audit_summary.json` compares all 804 valid/test rows at seven levels. The replay's `surface_audit_summary.json` additionally checks actual historical requests, common tools and progressive prefixes. `replacement_review.json` is a separate gate on newly selected evidence; inference cannot start without it. Broader coverage remains incomplete and these gates do not certify an error-free library.

Replay root: `outputs/paper/starling_conditioned_dili_retrieval_review_v4_no_prior_grounded_sim0`. The existing shared runner uses Hosted Flash-0731, 256 concurrent requests per mode, 6-way racing retries and a 20480 completion cap. It recomputes only changed independent inputs and affected progressive suffixes; common tools and single/None are frozen. Gold, labels, query identity visibility, prior, similarity, 4/2 budget and progressive reasoning are unchanged. Source decisions were not chosen by benchmark performance. Carcinogens and other tasks are untouched.

The source-coverage gate rejects superseded raw surfaces even when previously read. `review_coverage_validation.json` checks rejection of nine known erroneous old cards and acceptance of an unchanged voter bookkeeping surface plus the corrected 4PBA card. The runner uses its existing preparation/inference implementation with only its process-local artifact root pointed at the staged scaffold build; no shared runner or other task configuration is changed.
