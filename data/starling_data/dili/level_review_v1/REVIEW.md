# DILI progressive level record review

Reviewed 69 distinct complete canonical/raw payloads: base 14 and each v1–v5 11. Selection used fixed UID-hash ranks within source × random, clinical/direct-mention, and identity-held strata; overlaps were removed. Six actual voters (three of each label) were added. These are Codex semantic readings, not human expert review or a systematic full-paper audit. Two existing base-vote payloads were additionally re-read to resolve cross-source study duplication.

The 69 placement decisions are L1 3, L2 23, L3 3, L4 7, L5 4, L6 3, L7 3, excluded 23. Exclusions conserve source records. Twelve are existing structural/material holds; additional exclusions include identity ambiguity and explicit nonhepatic endpoints. These targeted counts do not estimate corpus prevalence.

L1 follows actual study-vote provenance. L2 contains all model-visible direct disclosures, including clinical or predicted outcomes, negatives, protective/challenge roles, mixed cell/clinical passages and in-vivo liver outcome markers. A DILI-patient-derived cell line does not confer a new human study vote. Viral hepatitis as baseline disease alone is not DILI. Families L3–L7 follow the measured endpoint before platform/background, with no source-run fallback. For example, immunofluorescent lysosome staining is L7, not an immune mechanism; liver bile-acid metabolomics is L4. Explicit lung, neural, embryo, leukemia and endothelial-only experiments without hepatic endpoints remain conserved but do not enter task-specific retrieval.

Three sampled actual votes require withdrawal because the reporting PMID is a review/letter rather than an original independent study: rimegepant 35329982 (many acute/preventive/long-term trials), rivaroxaban 20617417 (pooled phase II/III development trials), acetaminophen 34937482 (letter discussing a cited 14-case series). Hash-bound decisions are in gold_review_updates.jsonl. No general expansion of gold eligibility is made.

Two mechanism-source positive clinical recall leads (INH 24397791 and APAP 29056914) already have corresponding same-parent/study base votes. Full re-reading of those base payloads confirmed richer clinical information than the mechanism extracts. They must not increase the number of independent study votes. Exact snapshots and reasons are in gold_recall_pending.jsonl; its status records study duplication, not acceptance of an additional vote.

Five identity review holds are recorded. Rilpivirine has a cabotegravir-like structure and a nonempty name, permitting exact name+SMILES matching. Four mechanism records have blank canonical names and ambiguous/mismatched names in extra_details (NFT/nocardicin-F nitro claim, DAPT/polycarboxylate chelator, BCH/GDH-activator expansion, CHX/chlorpromazine scaffold); these use record-level holds to avoid suppressing legitimate different named uses of the same structure. Source identity must be resolved before release; no corrected structure is invented. PubChem independently describes gamma-secretase inhibitor DAPT as a fluorinated dipeptide (https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Dapt), incompatible with the supplied chelator. A primary hepatocyte study identifies the conventional GDH-activating BCH as an aminobicycloheptane carboxylic acid (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021925818669844); the current source expansion is held pending original-name confirmation. PubMed access for the exact CHX/BCH papers did not return usable content, so those remain unresolved identity holds rather than claimed full-paper verification.

Validation: 12 targeted tests pass in tests/chembl_tool/common/test_dili_starling_levels.py, covering direct negatives/predictions/protectors, actual voter-only L1, original identity holds, baseline viral-hepatitis specificity, nonhepatic scope, endpoint-before-platform assignment, source independence, all review payload hashes and no reviewed direct disclosure left in L3–L7. The shared builder performs full-corpus conservation and heldout/index checks separately.

Follow-up withdrawal audit: read all 11 removed vote payloads and their conditions after the shared bracketed-reference gate repair. Nine withdrawals are secondary/pooled clinical reports. Two original reports were falsely removed because collateral citations coexist with primary observations: allopurinol PMID 2925064 (original six-case registry histology analysis) and carbimazole PMID 12201214 (original dechallenge/rechallenge case). Their primary PubMed abstracts were checked; restore_review_updates.jsonl preserves their original Y/condition/study. The three placement-audited retained L1 records remain actual voters. No new explicit answer-bearing conditions were identified in this 11-record difference.
