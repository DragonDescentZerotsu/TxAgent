# Carcinogens record semantic review v1

72 supplied records were read individually, including every field in the complete `raw_record_json`. This is Codex source-text semantic review, not human-expert review or verification of original papers. Sampling used 12 distinct reporting PMIDs from each of base/v1/v2/v3/v4/v5, targeting actual voters, direct assertions, negative phrases, mechanism-only evidence and existing identity exclusions. It is a targeted error-discovery sample, not an estimate of error prevalence.

`review_sample.jsonl` preserves the complete canonical/source snapshots. `placement_decisions.jsonl` contains each permanent UID, canonical payload hash and individual reasoning. Shared builders must validate hashes before applying a decision. `identity_holds.jsonl` provides precisely scoped input holds; wrong-name records sharing otherwise legitimate structures require the claimed name in source text. `gold_review_updates.jsonl` withdraws the falsely independent AFB1 study vote and one separately reviewed oxaliplatin complex-to-diamine identity vote; `vote_recall_review.jsonl` records 12 examined recall candidates, none sufficiently established to add a new vote.

The seven families are L1 actual study voters, L2 nonvoter carcinogenicity/tumour assertions, L3 transformation and growth escape, L4 genotoxicity/DNA damage and repair, L5 reactive metabolism/redox injury, L6 epigenetic/intercellular growth restraint, and L7 regenerative/receptor-driven growth. Membership follows record endpoint semantics across source runs. Entire cards containing direct assertions are retained in L2 even when the primary assay is mechanistic, rather than trimming away inconvenient source text. Cancer cell-line identity alone does not establish direct carcinogenicity.

The review found explicit material/complex-to-free-ligand errors, wrong molecular assignments, genotype-only comparisons incorrectly attached to an indexed molecule, and motor behaviour incorrectly described as regeneration. These require identity or attribution holds. It also found tumour suppression by protective agents, site-only negatives, transplanted-cell tumour growth and secondary risk assertions: such passages are useful with their role/context preserved but cannot become new overall-hazard votes.

The one withdrawn AFB1 vote described a two-year rat experiment but explicitly stated that the result was cited as `reference [15]`; the reporting PMID had been incorrectly used as an original study. The original study must be resolved before voting. The reviewed DMBA C57BL bg/bg and +/bg row remains one vote because its existing model atom already preserves the pooled genotype context.

Six focused classifier tests cover actual-voter L1 membership, full-card direct containment, site negatives and xenografts, cancer cell-line names, cross-source family assignment and rejection of domain-only motor behaviour. The aggregate split/index validation is owned by the shared progressive build.

The coordinating agent additionally read actual oxaliplatin voter `sr_742bbc4cd272415dbd4cbcca1945a1aa` and confirmed the parent is an isolated diamine rather than the coordination complex. That second gold withdrawal is recorded in the same ledger and is additional to the 72-record sample.

A subsequent complete rereading of all 57 votes removed by the citation-rule repair found four false withdrawals: zero-dose `reference (0 Gy)` was a comparator, while urethane/MNU/AOM records cited model methods or limitations but reported their own experiments. `restore_review_updates.jsonl` records four hash-bound primary-study restorations, including removal of outcome-bearing relative risks/confidence intervals and preservation of pooled genotype/co-exposure conditions. The remaining 53 withdrawals comprise 48 secondary/nonindependent outcomes, four unresolved original-study/cohort origins and one identity failure. Both L1 UIDs in the original 72-record placement sample remain current. Across the sample and this regression audit, 128 unique complete source records were read.
